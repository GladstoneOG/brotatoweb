# 🥔 Brotato Web + Online Multiplayer

Play **Brotato** directly in your browser with real 1–4 player online co-op!

### 🎮 [Play Online Now](https://brotatoweb.johansans999.workers.dev)
**Live Link:** [https://brotatoweb.johansans999.workers.dev](https://brotatoweb.johansans999.workers.dev)

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌐 **1–4 Player Online Co-op**: Play together with real online room browsing or 1-click shareable invite links.
- 💾 **Automatic Local Saves**: Your progress, unlocks, and settings persist automatically on your computer.
- 🔄 **Steam Save Compatibility**: Easily export or import your vanilla `save_v3_0.json` Steam save file.
- ⚡ **Instant Repeat Loading**: Game files are cached in your browser on first launch, so future visits load in seconds.
- 🎮 **Full Controller & Keyboard Support**: Plug in a gamepad or play with standard WASD / Arrow keys.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play with Friends

1. Open **[brotatoweb.johansans999.workers.dev](https://brotatoweb.johansans999.workers.dev)** in your browser.
2. Click **Create Lobby** on the Main Menu (or use **➕ Host New Room** in the top bar).
3. Have your friends click **Join Lobby** from the Main Menu, or click **🔗 Copy Invite Link** in the top bar and send it to them!

---

## ⌨️ Controls & Shortcuts

- **Movement:** `WASD` or `Arrow Keys`
- **Pause Game:** `Esc` *(stays in fullscreen!)*
- **Toggle Fullscreen:** `F11`
- **Top Utility Bar:** `F2` to toggle the top menu (rooms, saves, fullscreen)
